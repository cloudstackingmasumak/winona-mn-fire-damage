Do Not Forget About Your Belongings in Other Locations

Many people focus on their home but forget about items in storage units, at dry cleaners, or at repair shops when fire damage occurs. Fire damage can affect items stored elsewhere if the facility itself is damaged. Make sure your insurance adjuster knows about all your belongings and their locations.

For items at off-site locations, you may need to arrange professional pickup and restoration separately from your home restoration. Coordinating this takes planning and attention to detail. In Winona, items that are forgotten in the immediate aftermath sometimes remain in storage indefinitely, a tragedy for irreplaceable possessions.

Damaged insulation, old flooring materials, or paint on surfaces in your Winona home might contain asbestos or lead. Disturbing these materials without professional guidance creates serious risks. You cannot tell by looking whether materials contain these hazardous substances.

Hard flooring being retained rather than replaced needs protection from dirt, dust, and potential chemical spills from restoration materials. Heavy plastic sheeting protects floors during active work. Cardboard overlays in high-traffic areas where equipment moves repeatedly protect flooring from damage by wheels, dropped tools, and heavy equipment. Professional crews remove these protective layers carefully at completion to avoid damaging the floors they protected. Countertops and other surfaces not being replaced need covering. Cardboard, plywood, or multi-layer protection shields surfaces from potential impact damage, chemical spills, and debris. Careful application prevents moisture trapping against surfaces. Professional teams' Winona crews remove protection in proper sequence so that newly protected surfaces don't become stained or damaged during the removal process.
